## Bus stops

Find stops near a location or by name, then load live arrivals into
`BusArrivalsBoard` (flat, time-ordered by default). Uses `stopPoint.getByGeoPoint`,
`stopPoint.search`, and `stopPoint.getArrivals`.

The installable Interface is [Bus Arrivals](/docs/bus-arrivals) — pass
normalised predictions as `data`. Keep discovery (location / search) in your
app or this Explorer view. The explorer composition is `BusArrivals`; the
board is `BusArrivalsBoard`.
