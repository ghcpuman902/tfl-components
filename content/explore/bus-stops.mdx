## Bus stops

Find stops near a location or by name, then load live arrivals into the shared
`ArrivalsBoard` (`variant="bus"`). Uses `stopPoint.getByGeoPoint`,
`stopPoint.search`, and `stopPoint.getArrivals`.

The installable Interface is [Bus Arrivals](/docs/bus-arrivals) — pass
normalised predictions as `data`. Keep discovery (location / search) in your
app or this Explorer view.
